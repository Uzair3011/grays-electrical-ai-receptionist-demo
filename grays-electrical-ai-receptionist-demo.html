Prefer to read it? A full sample call

Word for word what Grace says on a landlord EICR call. The live demo above follows this same script.

Grace

Hello, Grays Electrical and Compliance, you are speaking with Grace, the AI receptionist. I can help with EICRs, rewires, EV chargers, fuse box upgrades and emergencies. What do you need help with today?

Customer

Hi, I need an EICR for a house I rent out in Hartlepool.

Grace

Of course, I can book that in for you. Is that for a property you rent out, a house you are buying or selling, or your own home?

Customer

I rent it out. The tenant is moving out next week, so it is empty at the moment.

Grace

That is helpful, thank you. Can I take your name?

Customer

Sarah Thompson.

Grace

Thanks, Sarah. And the best mobile number to reach you on? The engineer will confirm everything by text.

Customer

07700 900123.

Grace

Perfect. Here are the next available slots for the visit. Which suits you best?

Customer

Tomorrow morning, 9 to 11.

Grace

Let me read that back. EICR / landlord certificate for Sarah Thompson. Details: rented property in Hartlepool, currently empty. Booked for tomorrow, 9:00 to 11:00, and we will text confirmation to 07700 900123. Shall I book that in?

Customer

Yes please.

Grace

All booked in. Your reference is GE-4821. You will get a confirmation text shortly, and Joe's team has the full details. Thanks for calling Grays Electrical. Anything else I can help with?

On emergencies the script changes order: safety advice first (switch off at the consumer unit, keep away from anything damaged), then name and number, then a priority callback for Joe with no slot offered. Try it in the call demo above.
